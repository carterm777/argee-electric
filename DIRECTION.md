# Design direction — Argee Electric

Assigned so that no two of the 25 sites in this campaign converge on the same
look. These are **starting positions with authority**, not idle suggestions: build
from them. You may deviate from any one of them, but the deviation costs you one
line of written justification in `SELECTION-LOG.md` explaining what the assigned
option lost against and why — the same discipline `section-style-repo.md` asks
for everywhere else.

**Primary style family:** textured-organic
**Page ambition level:** mixed

## The specific risk on this brief

Maroon and brown with Coustard — a heavy, warm slab serif. This is the family-shop site: paper, ink, letterpress weight. Grain and printed-matter detailing, not gloss. Avoid anything glassy or blurred.

## Signature element — the photo-diagnosis widget

Warm Illustrated Widget Frame — the uploader sits on a paper card with a printed-form rhythm

This is the most designed thing on the page. If a stranger screenshotted one
element from this site, it should be this one.

## Carry these through the page

- **Image technique to feature:** Tilted or Rotated Photo Card — from `references/image-and-visual-richness.md`. Use it at least twice, deliberately.
- **Motion technique to feature:** Bordered Frame Draw around each photograph — from `references/animation-and-motion-richness.md`.

Both are on top of, not instead of, the per-section choices above. Read the real
entry in the reference file before implementing either — the Avoid-when field
applies to you.
